## 👋 About Me

I'm a software developer from Germany focused on building modern web applications, APIs and developer tools.

I enjoy taking awkward or data-heavy systems and turning them into products that are easier to understand, integrate with and use. A large part of my personal work revolves around **Minecraft Bedrock, Marketplace data, PlayFab and real-time services**.

- 🎓 Completed my apprenticeship in **Application Development at FUNKE Mediengruppe** in 2026.
- 💻 Primarily working with **Vue.js, JavaScript, Go and Node.js**.
- 🔌 Building APIs, authentication systems, real-time features and third-party integrations.
- 📊 Interested in turning complex datasets into useful interfaces and services.
- ⛏️ Developing tools around the **Minecraft Bedrock Marketplace and PlayFab ecosystem**.

### ✦ Highlights

- **View Marketplace** started as a better way to browse Minecraft Marketplace data and has grown into a larger platform around releases, creators, sales and catalog information.
- Built much of the infrastructure behind it, including **APIs, authentication, real-time streams, alerts and Discord integrations**.
- Some of my tools are already being used by **Minecraft Marketplace creators and partners** in their day-to-day work.
- I care about building technical products that are powerful behind the scenes while still being **simple and pleasant to use**.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🌐 [View Marketplace](https://view-marketplace.net)

A platform for exploring **Minecraft Bedrock Marketplace releases, creators, sales and catalog data** without digging through several different places.

`Vue.js` · `JavaScript` · `REST APIs` · `Real-time Events`

- Browse Marketplace releases, creators, sales and catalog information in one place.
- Save items to wishlists and receive alerts through the website or Discord.
- Track Marketplace changes and discover newly released or discounted content.
- Open supported content directly in Minecraft.
- Designed around making Marketplace data easier to explore and understand.

### 🔌 [PlayFab Catalog Service — Bedrock](https://github.com/Daniel-Ric/PlayFab-Catalog-Service-Bedrock)

An open-source API that turns Minecraft Bedrock Marketplace data from **PlayFab** into a cleaner interface for searching, monitoring and integrating with the catalog.

`Node.js` · `Express` · `OpenAPI` · `SSE` · `Webhooks`

- Advanced catalog search, filtering, pagination and enriched results.
- Monitors **items, prices, sales and creator changes**.
- Streams updates through **Server-Sent Events**.
- Supports **signed webhook notifications** for external services.
- Includes JWT authentication, caching, rate limiting and ETags.
- Provides a documented **OpenAPI specification** for integrations.
